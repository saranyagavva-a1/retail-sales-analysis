Through data cleaning, cohort profiling, SQL aggregation, and Power BI visualization, this project isolates negative-margin product lines, evaluates discounting thresholds, and delivers actionable business recommendations.Key Metrics SummaryTotal Sales Revenue: $2,297,201Total Net Profit: $286,397Overall Net Margin: ~12.5%Total Orders Processed: 5,009Identified Annual Profit Leakage: ~$21,300+ (from heavy discounting)🚨 Business ProblemDespite achieving $2.30M+ in top-line revenue, Superstore's net profit margin remains suppressed at ~12.5%. Executive leadership noted significant margin compression across specific product categories and geographic regions.Core Challenges:Uncontrolled Promotional Discounting: Discounts as high as 70%–80%
